### 🏥 "In the ICU, seconds aren't just time. They are heartbeats."

SepsisGuard AI is not just software. It is a **predictive powerhouse** designed to hunt down sepsis before it even shows its face. Built with a "Clinical-First" philosophy, this platform transforms chaotic bedside data into crystal-clear, actionable intelligence.

---

## 🔥 Why SepsisGuard is KILLER:

*   **⚡ 8.5h Early Detection Advantage:** While standard protocols wait for symptoms, our AI analyzes biomarkers to give clinicians a half-day head start.
*   **🧪 The "What-If" Simulator:** A groundbreaking feature that allows doctors to simulate fluid boluses and vasopressor titration on a digital twin, predicting outcomes before they happen.
*   **🧠 Explainable AI (XAI):** We solve the "Black Box" problem. Every risk score comes with SHAP-driven insights—showing exactly which labs (Lactate, WBC, MAP) are driving the risk.
*   **📊 Global Analytics Engine:** Facility-wide population stratification. Track survival rate improvements (+14.2% in Q3) and model performance across all hospital departments.
*   **📋 Clinical Autopilot:** Automated SBAR summaries, one-click transfers, and a persistent **Global AI Assistant** that monitors every bed while the team is in surgery.
